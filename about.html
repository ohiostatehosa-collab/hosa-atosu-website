⛰️ About Us

Mission, vision & values

Who we are and what we're building at HOSA @ Ohio State.

🧭 Mission
To empower Ohio State students to become compassionate, skilled, and ethical future health professionals through leadership development, hands-on service, and experiential learning that advances health locally and globally.
🔍 Vision
To build a premier collegiate HOSA chapter that shapes the next generation of health leaders for students who are innovative, service-driven, and committed to improving health equity and patient outcomes across communities worldwide.
⚖️ Values

Leadership with Purpose

We develop leaders who take initiative, act with integrity, and serve with humility on campus, in healthcare settings, and beyond.

Service that Matters

We believe service should create real impact. Our projects are intentional, community-centered, and aligned with improving health and wellbeing for underserved populations.

Health Equity & Advocacy

We are committed to addressing health disparities and expanding access to care through education, advocacy, and partnership.

Professional Excellence

We hold ourselves to high standards in preparation, conduct, and collaboration, striving for excellence in academics, competitions, and real-world healthcare experiences.

Inclusive Community

We foster a welcoming, supportive environment where students from all backgrounds can grow, lead, and succeed together in the health professions.

Growth Through Experience

We learn by doing through competitions, service, mentorship, and hands-on opportunities that prepare us for the realities of healthcare.

Global Perspective

We recognize health as a global responsibility and engage with initiatives that broaden our understanding of healthcare systems and challenges worldwide.
