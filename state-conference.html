Workshop 1

Project Shokti — An International Hands-On Service Opportunity

  • Presented by: OSU HOSA Service Committee E-Board
  • Summary: Assemble care kits for mothers in Kolkata, India through OSU HOSA's signature service project, Project Shokti.
  • Focus: Raising awareness of period poverty and supporting maternal health and wellness.
  • Activity: Members will form teams to assemble the most care packages (creative designs and personal touches encouraged). Prizes will be awarded to the winning team.
  • Outcome: Assembled boxes will be shipped to women in Kolkata.
Workshop 2

Bias in Healthcare

Unconscious bias can influence clinical decisions, workplace culture, communication, and patient outcomes — even among highly trained professionals. This interactive workshop recognizes common bias patterns, reflects on how they appear in real clinical settings, and practices strategies to support equitable, team-centered, and patient-focused care. Participants leave with practical tools they can immediately apply in their daily living to combat unconscious biases.
