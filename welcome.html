FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is HOSA @ Ohio State?

HOSA @ Ohio State is a student-led organization for students interested in healthcare and the health professions. We focus on leadership development, service, professional growth, and hands-on learning through events, competitions, and community impact.

Who can join HOSA @ Ohio State?

Any Ohio State student with an interest in healthcare is welcome — you do not have to be pre-med. We welcome students exploring medicine, nursing, public health, allied health, healthcare administration, and more.

Is there an application process to join?

No application is required. You can join at any time during the academic year by paying the annual membership dues.

How much are membership dues?
  • $5 chapter dues
  • $20 national HOSA membership fee
What kinds of events does HOSA offer?

We host:

  • Workshops and skill-building sessions
  • Healthcare speaker and networking events
  • Community service and volunteering opportunities
  • HOSA competitive event preparation
  • Social and team-building events
Do I have to compete in HOSA events?

No. Competitions are optional. Members can participate in service, leadership, workshops, and networking without competing.

How much time commitment is required?

HOSA is flexible. You can choose your level of involvement based on your schedule, with opportunities ranging from occasional events to leadership roles.

Can freshmen join?

Yes! Freshmen are highly encouraged to join and get involved early.

Are there leadership opportunities?

Yes. Members can apply for:

  • Executive Board positions
  • Committee Head roles
  • Subcommittee leadership roles
